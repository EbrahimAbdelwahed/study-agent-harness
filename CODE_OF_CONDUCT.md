# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community for contributors,
reviewers, maintainers, and users.

Expected behaviour includes communicating clearly and in good faith, respecting
different backgrounds and levels of experience, focusing critique on the work
and its evidence, and protecting private study material and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and
publishing private or sensitive data are unacceptable.

Report a conduct concern through an available private repository channel. If no
private channel is configured, open a public issue containing only a request for
a private contact path; do not include incident details. Maintainers will limit
access to reports and act to protect participants where possible.
