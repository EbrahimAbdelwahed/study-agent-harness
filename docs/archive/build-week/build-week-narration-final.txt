STUDY AGENT HARNESS — BUILD WEEK VOICE-OVER
Target: 80 seconds. Natural, calm, confident. Do not read the scene labels.

Most study tools make the student adapt to a workflow. A real tutor does the opposite.

[00:07–00:22]

[00:22–00:38]
Before it teaches, the harness locks a trusted snapshot of your source. The tutor can ask the one question that matters next—“Which valve should we focus on?”—and use your answer to change direction without losing context.

[00:38–00:57]
Now the lesson becomes a loop. Explain one idea from the source. Test it immediately. Record what the student understood, and schedule what should return later. The conversation stays flexible; the learning state keeps accumulating.

[00:57–01:12]
Underneath, every transition is an append-only event. Pause, resume, or change models: the same continuation can be replayed and verified. Skills carry the teaching behavior. Provider adapters stay replaceable.
