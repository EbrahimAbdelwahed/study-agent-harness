# Slice 04: Study artifact proposals

## Outcome

Grounded flashcards, assessment items, exam blueprints, and study briefs can be
proposed, revised, accepted, rejected, and superseded without generated content
approving itself.

## Contract

- Kind-specific strict codecs sit behind one artifact envelope.
- Proposal provenance pins sources, prompt, model, validators, skill, playbook,
  and prior revision when applicable.
- Human or explicit trusted host policy decides acceptance.
- Domain procedures for generating flashcard proposals and analyzing exam
  samples ship with grounding and adversarial fixtures. Their gateway
  capability manifests are registered only after TUT-03 and are integrated by
  TUT-06.
