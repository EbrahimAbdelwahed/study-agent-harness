# KB-17: Figure state, structural anchors, and review

Status: Proposed parent — child beads own implementation
Risk: High
Depends On: KB-03, KB-05, KB-14, KB-16
Parent coverage: §§9.1, 9.5, 9.8, 13; M7

## Outcome

Figures are content-addressed uniform units with exact/generated/derived anchors,
and human review decisions remain canonical across extractor reruns.

## Child beads

- [KB-17A](KB-17A-figure-units-structural-anchors.md): content-addressed figure
  units plus exact/generated anchors.
- [KB-17B](KB-17B-derived-pdf-anchors.md): bounded page-geometry derived anchors
  and confidence.
- [KB-17C](KB-17C-anchor-review-correspondence.md): canonical review authority,
  rerun reconciliation, and declared cross-document correspondence.

## Out of scope

- OCR, vision captions, figure search ranking, UI review surface, or image
  embeddings.
